# AC-03 · Prompts de imagen v0.1

Estilo base (usar en todos los planos):

> Semi-realistic editorial illustration, cinematic documentary tone, dark steel
> submarine interior, worn metal texture, fine dust, warm copper light against cold
> steel, high contrast, muted palette (#0B0F12, #8FA3B0, #B87333, #D9942F), 16:9,
> shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no weapons, no military insignia, no
> identifiable real faces.

Personaje (describir en cada prompt que aparezca):

> Fictional guide: man around 60, medium build, short grey beard, weathered face,
> dark worn work jacket, no insignia, calm attentive expression. Composite character,
> not a real person.

## Planos

**P1 — General del puesto central**
> Wide shot of a diesel-electric submarine central station at a museum: periscope
> columns, valve manifold with copper wheels, consoles, worn steel bulkheads. Warm
> copper practical lights, deep shadows, no people. 16:9. Negative: text, logos,
> weapons, torpedoes, military badges, bright render, plastic look.

**P2 — Guía de perfil**
> Medium profile shot of the fictional guide standing beside the periscope column,
> looking up slightly, copper rim light on his face, steel background. 16:9.
> Negative: identifiable real person, uniform, insignia, text, logos.

**P3 — Columna del periscopio**
> Close-up detail of a periscope column and its lenses inside a museum submarine,
> brass and steel, grease and wear, soft copper light. Vertical motion implied. 16:9.

**P4 — Vista por el periscopio**
> View through a submarine periscope: calm sea surface and sky at dusk, horizon line,
> slight vignette and lens edge, documentary realism, no ships, no weapons. 16:9.

**P5 — Colector de válvulas**
> Close-up of a ballast valve manifold: copper handwheels, pipes, pressure gauges,
> worn paint, warm light, dark steel. 16:9.

**P6 — Manos sobre el volante**
> Detail shot of weathered working hands turning a copper valve wheel, shallow depth
> of field, dust in the light. 16:9.

**P7 — Timón y planos**
> Medium shot of the helm station: rudder wheel and depth-plane controls, brass and
> steel, dim instrument glow, no people or double hands only. 16:9.

**P8 — Instrumentos**
> Close-up of submarine navigation instruments: compass repeater, depth gauge, glass
> and brass, warm glow, worn panel. 16:9.

**P9 — Guía junto al periscope**
> Medium shot of the fictional guide beside the periscope, gesturing calmly toward the
> mechanism, copper light, steel background, documentary illustration. 16:9.

**P10 — Tarjeta ISA**
> Dark background with subtle grid and a single copper light streak; space for the ISA
> logo and CTA text added in post. 16:9. No text baked in.

## Notas

- Mantener el mismo personaje y la misma campera en P2, P6, P9.
- No generar texto en la imagen: los subtítulos y títulos van en post.
- Evitar cualquier objeto que parezca arma, munición o insignia militar.
- Para Shorts, reencuadrar a 9:16 manteniendo el sujeto en el tercio superior.
